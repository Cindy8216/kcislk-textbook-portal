Summer Reading List

暑假閱讀推薦書單 · IPD Teachers' Picks

Dear Kang Chiao Linkou students,

None of these books will be on a test. We, your teachers, will not create projects, assign homework or attach any other means of academic or personal responsibility to the reading of these texts. This is a list of books that have moved us, inspired us, and opened our eyes to lives lived other than our own. The purpose of this list is to share our love of reading with you and maybe in doing so, you may find that you too are interested in stories, fiction and nonfiction.

You don't have to read all of them. You may not read any of them, but perhaps you will find a book here that isn't too long, too boring, or too difficult. Maybe you will find a book that seems like it was written just for you in this time and place. We hope to hear about your reading journey when we meet again. Have a great summer.

— Your Teachers

The Books

Important Information

領取說明 Distribution

G7–G10 課本由學校統一訂購並於開學首日發放。G11–G12 「英文科」由學校採購；其餘科目採預購制（表單於 5 月公告）。

G7–G10 textbooks are distributed on the first day. G11–G12 English is school-purchased; others are pre-order (available in May).

退換說明 Returns

預購書籍恕不接受退書。其他課本退換請於開學後兩週內至國際處 IPD 辦理，逾期不予受理。

Pre-ordered textbooks are non-returnable. Other returns must be processed at the IPD Office within the first 2 weeks.

課程使用時程 Usage Period

除了英文科讀本會分上下學期使用外，其餘課本皆使用一學年！

Textbooks are for the full year, except for English Readers which are issued by semester.

收費參考 Pricing Info

目前網頁顯示的是本學年 (SY25-26) 的金額，僅供預算參考；新學年 (SY26-27) 正式金額將在 7 月更新。

Prices shown are SY25-26 rates for reference only; official SY26-27 fees will be updated in July.

詳細明細 More Info

如需查閱詳細書目資訊（作者、版本等），請點選該書目之「ISBN」連結。

For detailed bibliographic info (editions, authors, etc.), please click the "ISBN" link.

貼心提醒 Notice

本校保留根據教學需求調整選書之權利。

The school reserves the right to adjust textbook selections based on instructional requirements.
